What it does

Built around the protocol you already get

Enter results from the official score sheet once — SkaterProgress does the math and keeps the history.

Competitions & programs

Short and free programs for every competition, grouped by season.

Full element protocol

Elements, levels, GOE and flags like UR, q or << — just like on the score sheet.

TES, PCS & Total

Line charts across competitions show the trend, not just the last result.

Placements in context

Every place shown with the field size, best results on top.

Strengths at a glance

Top elements and strongest categories, calculated from your own data.

Offline, in two languages

Works without internet. Available in English and Ukrainian.

How it works

Three steps after every competition

Create a skater profile

Name, date of birth and level — that’s all it takes to start.

Add the competition

Enter the program, elements and PCS from the official protocol.

Watch the progress

Charts, placements and strengths update automatically.

Private by design

Your skater’s data stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, nothing to sign up for. You decide who sees the results.

  • No accounts or registration
  • No ads, analytics or trackers
  • All data stored only on your device
  • Delete the app — and the data is gone
I built SkaterProgress because I wanted to see my daughter’s progress from season to season — not just the score from the last event.

As a parent of a young figure skater, I kept protocols in photos, notes and spreadsheets. SkaterProgress is the app I wished I had: simple enough to fill in at the rink, detailed enough for a real conversation with the coach.
