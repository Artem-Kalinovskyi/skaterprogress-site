# Data Deletion

SkaterProgress stores data only locally on your device.

The app does not store personal data on external servers, so the developer has no access to your data and cannot delete or restore it.

To delete all data, use any of these options:

- in the app: **Profile → Delete profile and all data**;
- uninstall the app from your device;
- or clear the app's data: **Android Settings → Apps → SkaterProgress → Storage → Clear data**.
